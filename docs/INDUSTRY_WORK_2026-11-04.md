## 1. What each business now gets

Each industry has a set of **capabilities**:
- Bookings, Check-ins, Waitlist
- Dispatch
- Classes
- Orders
- Money (estimates, invoices, deposits, fees and gift cards the team records)
- Access codes

The industry decides two things:
- which capabilities start **on**;
- which ones the owner **can switch on** later.

Everything else is not offered.

When a capability is off:
- its pages leave the menu, Today and the header;
- the database refuses new work of that kind, whoever asks (staff, the AI receptionist, integrations);
- nothing already recorded is deleted.

The owner's choices are stored apart from the defaults, and new defaults never overwrite them.

The capability matrix is generated by `docs/generators/industry_tables.py` from the migrations themselves. Some types are tied to a capability: classes, orders and jobs refuse new records while it is off.

**How existing businesses are upgraded.** The preset upgrade is `zella_apply_crm_preset`. It runs in the migration and again each time the CRM opens.

What it never does:
- removes or renames a business's fields or types;
- re-adds a field the business once had (`fields_seen`);
- changes a field's type;
- touches types the business created itself.

What it does:
- New **fields** are added to preset types.
- New **types** arrive switched **off** for businesses already on that industry; the owner turns them on under Customize records.
- A business that is new to the preset gets them on. That covers new businesses, and insurance and cleaning businesses moving to their own presets.
- Privacy only goes up. A field the preset marks private becomes private, and its values move out of normal record data.

Two splits:
- **Insurance** gets its own preset: Policies, Claims (first notice of loss), Quotes, Policy requests.
- **Cleaning** is split from home services: Properties with checklist and private access codes, Cleaning visits, Cleaning plans.

Old types that hold records stay; empty ones are switched off.

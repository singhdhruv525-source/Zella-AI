## 7. Launch blockers (owner actions before launch)

Zella Voice has not launched. The public legal documents describe each control as the service will provide it at launch. Before any public document is published, the owner must make sure each item below is built and verified in the product, or have the promise removed from the public text (the counsel memo, section 4, gives where each promise appears). Each production change needs the owner's explicit approval for that specific change (NN 3, NN 4). Each production change needs the owner's explicit approval for that specific change (NN 3, NN 4). Card masking (action 11) is required before any live call regardless of the public text, because Zella Voice's policy is that card details are never collected or stored. **Exception for recording (action 1):** removing the public promise is not an option. Call recording must stay disabled for every Customer until recording consent and caller disclosures have passed legal review, whatever the public text says, and the live Vapi recording setting must be confirmed, for the account and for each assistant, and the result recorded with the date and the person who checked.

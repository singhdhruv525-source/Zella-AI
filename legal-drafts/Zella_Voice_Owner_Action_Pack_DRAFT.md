### 1.7 Using TM and ®

- "TM" may be used with ZELLA VOICE at any time to claim the mark.
- "®" may be used only after the USPTO registers the mark, and only for the registered goods and services. Using it earlier can harm the application.
- Write the brand as "Zella Voice" in full, consistently, as the rulebook requires.

## 6. Counsel engagement brief

### 6.1 What to ask a SaaS lawyer to review, in priority order

1. TCPA consent for AI voice calls and texts, and the consent wording (Consent Pack, Messaging Terms).
2. Call recording disclosure and all party consent states, before recording is ever turned on.
3. Platform liability for AI drafts that a Customer approves, including Section 230.
4. The liability cap, floor and carve outs in the Terms.
5. Indemnities in both directions.
6. Governing law, forum, arbitration, the class action and jury waiver, and the time limit for claims.
7. Retention periods, including the 30 or 90 day conflict after cancellation.
8. Whether to keep the EEA, UK and India sections, and the transfer mechanisms.
9. The HIPAA option and the BAA, only after vendor BAAs.
10. Messaging Terms and 10DLC registration, including the STOP and HELP mismatches, and the DMCA agent.

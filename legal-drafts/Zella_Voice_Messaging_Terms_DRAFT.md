## 1. About these Messaging Terms

1.1 These Messaging Terms apply to text messages (SMS) and emails that a business using Zella Voice (the "Business") sends to you through the Zella Voice platform. Each Business runs its own messaging program; Zella Voice provides the technology that sends the messages for the Business.

1.2 Part A applies to people who receive messages. Part B applies to Customers that send messages through the Services, and forms part of the Agreement under section 1.5 of the Zella Voice Terms of Service ("Terms"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in section 2 of the Terms.

1.3 **Who emails come from.** An email from a Business comes from the Business's own email address, either on an email domain the Business has verified or through a Gmail or Outlook mailbox the Business has connected. Otherwise it is sent in the Business's name from a Zella Voice no reply address, with replies directed to the Business's email address. Replies go to the Business, not to Zella Voice.

## Part A. Terms for message recipients

## 2. Program description

2.1 **What you may receive.** Depending on what you ask for and what the Business has set up, you may receive:

- appointment confirmations, reminders, reschedule and cancellation confirmations, and links to change your booking;
- a text after you call the Business and the call is missed **[COUNSEL DECISION ON THE MISSED CALL BASIS: APPROVED OR REJECTED; IF REJECTED, DELETE THIS ITEM AND THE MISSED CALL TEXT IN SECTION 4.2]**, and replies to your messages;
- follow ups about your inquiry or a service you received;
- waitlist offers when a time frees up;
- marketing messages, such as offers or suggestions of related services, only if you have separately agreed to receive marketing messages from that Business.

2.2 **Program name.** Messages identify the Business that sends them. The program name is **[PROGRAM NAME, PER BUSINESS CAMPAIGN]**.

## 3. How you agree to receive messages

3.1 You agree to receive messages from a Business when you give it your phone number or email address and consent, for example by ticking an unticked box on its website or lead form, by agreeing during a call with its AI Receptionist before any confirmation text is sent, or by texting the Business first.

3.2 Consent to marketing messages is separate from consent to service messages. **Consent is not a condition of purchase.**

3.3 A typical website consent reads: "I agree that {business} may contact me by phone call, including calls using an automated or AI voice, and by text message at the number I provided, about my inquiry. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." **[COUNSEL TO APPROVE THE CONSENT WORDING]**

## 4. Message frequency and timing

4.1 **Message frequency varies** with your appointments and requests. The Services limit how often you are contacted: unless the Business's plan sets a different limit, no more than one unprompted message a day.

4.2 **Contact hours.** Messages are sent within contact hours in your local time zone when the Business knows it, and otherwise in the Business's time zone. Replies to your own messages, the missed call text and booking confirmations are sent immediately. The other windows are: follow ups after a call attempt, within the Business's calling hours; appointment reminders, between 7 a.m. and 9 p.m.; other service messages, between 9 a.m. and 7 p.m.; and marketing messages, between 9 a.m. and 7 p.m. A message due outside its window moves to the next allowed time. **[OWNER AND COUNSEL: CONFIRM THE HOUR RANGES, INCLUDING WHETHER MARKETING NEEDS A NARROWER WINDOW AND WHETHER REMINDERS NEED QUIET HOURS]**

## 5. Costs

**Message and data rates may apply.** Neither the Business nor Zella Voice charges you for messages, but your mobile carrier may.

## 6. How to stop messages

6.1 **Text STOP** to any message to stop texts from that Business. STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT all work. Your opt out applies to all texts from that Business, both service and marketing, and takes effect immediately. You receive one text confirming the opt out.

6.2 **Text START** (or UNSTOP or YES) to receive service texts from that Business again after opting out. Marketing texts stay stopped until you opt in to them again.

6.3 **Email.** Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email to stop marketing emails from that Business.

6.4 **Calls.** Tell the Business or its AI Receptionist "don't call me" to stop its calls, including automatic callbacks.

6.5 An opt out applies to the Business you opted out of. It does not stop messages from other businesses that use Zella Voice.

## 7. Help

Text **HELP** for help. The Business replies with its name, how to reach it and how to opt out. You can also contact the Business directly, or contact Zella Voice at **[SUPPORT CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## 8. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and device.

## 9. Privacy

9.1 The Business's own privacy policy explains how it uses your information. Zella Voice handles your information for the Business as described in the Zella Voice Privacy Policy at **[LEGAL DOCUMENTS LOCATION]**.

9.2 **Mobile information is not sold, and is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging opt in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver messages and run the Services for the Business. If you submit a lead form on Google or Meta, that platform collects your details under its own terms and passes your lead to the Business through Zella Voice; this is the platform sending your details in, and neither the Business nor Zella Voice shares your mobile information or consent back to those platforms for marketing. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THIS DISCLOSURE AGAINST THE GOOGLE AND META LEAD FORM FLOWS AND CARRIER REQUIREMENTS]**

## Part B. Terms for Customers that send messages

## 10. Customer obligations

10.1 **Consent.** Customer must obtain and record, before any message is sent, every consent the law and carrier rules require, separately for service and marketing messages. The Services record consent with when and how it was given, for which channel and for which purpose, and do not send a message without it.

10.2 **Registration.** Customer must register its brand and campaign for application to person texting (10DLC) or the equivalent, provide accurate sample messages and use cases, and send only messages that match the registered campaign. Texts to End Users start only after that registration is approved.

10.3 **Content.** Messages must identify the Customer, must not contain content that carriers prohibit, and must comply with Schedule 1 (Acceptable Use Policy) of the Terms. Marketing emails must include a working unsubscribe link and a valid physical postal address, and must not use false or misleading headers or subject lines.

10.4 **Templates.** Customer may edit message templates except protected compliance content, such as opt out instructions.

## 11. How the Services keep opt out records

11.1 Every consent and opt out is recorded per Business, with the channel, purpose, time, source and the keyword or link used. Consent records are kept as an append only history.

11.2 Before every message, the Services check consent, opt outs, do not contact flags, the frequency limit in section 4.1, the contact hours in section 4.2 and duplicate protection, at the time of sending, not only when the message was created.

11.3 When a Business erases a person's data, the person's consent and opt out history is kept so that the opt out continues to work.

11.4 Texts and emails are Live Features. Until Zella Voice switches them on for a Business, messages are recorded as "would have sent" and nothing is delivered.

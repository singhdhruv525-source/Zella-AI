## 9. Data subject requests

9.1 **Tools.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, Zella Voice will assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, to respond to requests from data subjects. The Services provide these tools:

- a request log in the CRM for access, export and erasure requests, each with a due date 30 days after it is recorded, and a daily reminder to the Owner before it is due;
- for one End User, a file of that person's records only (customer record, appointments, texts, emails, calls with summaries and transcripts, callbacks, consent history and requests), produced when an owner or admin carries out the logged request; as in every export, the transcripts of Customers in Regulated Industries are left out of the file;
- export of customers and call history as CSV at any time (the call history file holds no transcripts, summaries or recordings), and a whole business export on request, downloadable for 7 days, which leaves out recording links and the transcripts of Customers in Regulated Industries;
- correction of records in the CRM;
- erasure of an End User: their details, messages and calls, including transcripts, recordings and summaries, are removed, and for each call not under a legal hold, not named in a pending return request and not kept because the law requires it, deletion of the call at Vapi is requested. Consent and opt out history is retained so that opt outs continue to work, and data under a legal hold, including the copy of a held call at Vapi, is kept, and no deletion is requested at Vapi, until the hold is released (sections 12.4 and 12.5). A transcript or recording named in a pending return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1, including the copy at Vapi, is kept until it is returned, and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request (section 12.1), and is then deleted. Where such an item is also under a legal hold, section S1.6.3(e) of Schedule 1 applies. Call records the law requires to be kept are kept only for the legally required period and are then deleted (section 12.5(e)). Erasure needs the approval of a second owner or admin when the Customer has two or more active owners or admins; a Customer with only one owner or admin has no second approver, and the erasure is still recorded in the audit log;
- opt outs by STOP, unsubscribe link and "don't call me", honored immediately for Customer.

9.2 **Requests received by Zella Voice.** If Zella Voice receives a request from a data subject about Customer Personal Data, it will promptly forward it to Customer and will not respond except to direct the data subject to Customer, unless Customer instructs otherwise or the law requires.

## 12. Deletion or return

12.1 **During any Trial Period and the Subscription Term.** Customer Personal Data is retained and deleted as follows: call recordings (only when recording is on) and call transcripts are deleted 90 days after the call, or earlier when they are erased, at the end of the export window after cancellation, or on account deletion, except that a recording or transcript the law requires Zella Voice to keep is kept for the required period (section 12.5(e)), and except that deletion is suspended for a transcript or recording named in a pending return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1, until it is returned and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request (an item that is also under a legal hold, or also required by law to be kept, follows section S1.6.3(e) of Schedule 1); the contents of texts and emails are removed after 2 years, leaving only the record that a message was sent; and other Customer Personal Data is kept while the account is active, subject to Customer's own erasure.

12.2 **After cancellation or termination.** This section applies after every end of the Agreement, including termination, as section 12.6 of the Terms provides; where Zella Voice terminates under section 17.2 of the Terms, the export method and window are as set there. Customer Personal Data still held remains available for export, through the exports in section 9.1 and with the same exclusions (recording links, and transcripts of Customers in Regulated Industries, are not exported), for **[CANCELLATION RETENTION PERIOD]** after cancellation, after a final offer to export, and is then deleted. Each kind of data follows its own deletion schedule. The export window after cancellation covers only data still held at that time; it does not extend any shorter period, so a recording or transcript that reaches its 90 day deletion date during the window is deleted on that date and is not kept for export, unless it is named in a pending return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1, in which case it is kept, even past the end of the window, only until it is returned and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request (section 12.1); all other data is deleted at the end of the window. Backups are cleared within 90 days. The exceptions in section 12.5 apply.

12.3 **Account deletion.** On an account deletion request confirmed by Zella Voice, the account is frozen immediately and Customer Personal Data is deleted 30 days later unless the Owner withdraws the request. Stored files are removed, and deletion of the copies held by Vapi is requested and carried out where technically possible, as section 12.4 provides, except for data under a legal hold, which is kept, and not deleted at Vapi, until the hold is released. A transcript or recording named in a return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1 that is still pending when the 30 days end is not deleted then: as section 12.1 provides, it is kept until it is returned and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request, and is then deleted, including the copy held by Vapi. The exceptions in section 12.5 also apply; neither this section nor section 12.5 shortens the period for a pending return request.

12.4 **Provider copies.** Deletion extends to files and copies held by Subprocessors where technically possible, except for data under a legal hold (section 12.5), which is not deleted at a Subprocessor until the hold is released, and for a transcript or recording named in a pending return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1, whose copies at a Subprocessor are kept until it is returned, and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request (section 12.1), and are then deleted (where such an item is also under a legal hold or required by law to be kept, section S1.6.3(e) of Schedule 1 applies). Copies of data the law requires Zella Voice to keep (section 12.5(e)) are kept at a Subprocessor only for the required period, and are then deleted.

12.5 **Exceptions.** The deletions in sections 12.1 to 12.4 are subject to these exceptions. Zella Voice may retain: (a) the consent and opt out history for each telephone number or email address (channel, purpose, status, source and time), which is kept after erasure, cancellation and account deletion so that opt outs continue to work (section 9.1); (b) audit and security logs for 7 years, or longer if the law requires, which contain no message contents, transcripts or recordings and are kept without Customer's identifying details; (c) data under an active legal hold until the hold is released, after which it is deleted on schedule; (d) a transcript or recording named in a pending return request under section S1.6.3 of Schedule 1, until it is returned and for no longer than **[BAA RETURN PERIOD]** days after the request, as section 12.1 provides, after which it is deleted; where (d) and either (c) or (e) apply to the same item, section S1.6.3(e) of Schedule 1 decides, for every deletion in this section 12; and (e) data the law requires Zella Voice to keep. Retained data remains subject to this DPA. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THE RETENTION BASIS FOR CONSENT AND OPT OUT HISTORY AFTER CANCELLATION]**

12.6 **Certification.** On written request, Zella Voice will confirm in writing that deletion has been completed, and the confirmation will identify any copies held by Subprocessors that could not be deleted under section 12.4 and any data kept under the exceptions in section 12.5, with the reason for each and the expected deletion date where known.

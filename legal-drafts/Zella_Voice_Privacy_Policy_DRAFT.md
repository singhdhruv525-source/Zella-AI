## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

1.1 Zella Voice is an AI telephone receptionist and customer relationship management system for small businesses, such as restaurants, salons, clinics and trade services. It is provided by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company with its registered address at **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]** ("Zella Voice", "we", "us" or "our"). Zella Voice is currently offered to businesses in the United States.

1.2 Capitalized terms have the meanings given in section 2 of the Zella Voice Terms of Service. In particular, a "Customer" is a business that uses Zella Voice, an "Authorized User" is a person who signs in to Zella Voice for a Customer, and an "End User" is a caller, client, lead or other person who deals with a Customer through Zella Voice.

1.3 This policy explains how we handle Personal Data about:

- Authorized Users, including Owners;
- End Users; and
- visitors to our public pages, such as /status, /privacy and /terms, and people who open a private link we send, such as a reschedule or unsubscribe link.

1.4 Section 15 (cookies and browser storage) forms part of this policy. The full list of our service providers is in Annex 3 of the Zella Voice Data Processing Addendum and is also kept at **[SUBPROCESSOR LIST LOCATION]**.

## 2. Our role

2.1 **For End Users, the Customer is in charge.** The Customer decides why and how Personal Data about its End Users is used. For that data (Customer Personal Data), the Customer is the controller (in some laws, the "business" or "Data Fiduciary"), and Zella Voice processes it on the Customer's behalf as its processor and service provider under the Zella Voice Data Processing Addendum ("DPA"). If you are an End User, the Customer's own privacy notice applies to you, and you should contact the Customer first. We will help the Customer respond (section 12.1).

2.2 **For Authorized Users and visitors, we are in charge.** Zella Voice is the controller of Account Data and of Personal Data about visitors to our public pages.

## 3. Personal Data we collect

### 3.1 Account Data

- Name, email address, telephone number and role (owner, admin, manager, staff or viewer).
- Sign in records: password (held only in protected form by our sign in provider), two step sign in records, sign in times and signed in devices.
- Invitations, and acceptance of our legal documents (version, time and person).
- Owner identity verification: a photo identification document and the Customer's business registration, uploaded to private storage and checked by the Zella Voice team. Where enabled, an identity verification provider may carry out the check (section 8).
- Billing records: Plan, Fees, usage, invoices, refunds and payment status, and the brand, last 4 digits and expiry month and year of the saved payment card, which we receive from our payment processor, Stripe, so that billing notices can say which card will be charged (for example "your Visa ending 4242"). **Stripe holds the payment card. The full card number and security code of the saved payment card are entered only on Stripe's pages, and Zella Voice never receives or stores them.** The AI Receptionist never asks for, reads back or records card or bank details, and stops a caller who starts to give them; card details that a caller speaks on a call anyway, such as a card number, security code or expiry date, are masked in call transcripts and summaries before they are stored and are not kept in call recordings.
- Support requests and correspondence.

### 3.2 Customer Personal Data

A Customer keeps records about its End Users, which may include:

- names, telephone numbers, email addresses, preferred language and time zone;
- calls: calling and called numbers, time and length, what the AI Receptionist did, the outcome, a written summary and a transcript, and a recording only when the Customer has turned call recording on (section 6);
- texts and emails sent or received for the Customer, with delivery status and replies;
- appointments, reservations, jobs, waitlist entries, check ins and attendance;
- requests, notes, tasks, follow ups, quotes and recorded payments (never card details; see section 3.1);
- the records the Customer's industry uses, which may include uploaded files;
- relationships the Customer records, such as a parent or guardian;
- consent records, contact preferences, opt outs and do not contact flags;
- lead details from the Customer's website forms and chat widget, Google Ads lead forms and, where enabled, Facebook and Instagram lead ads; order details where a store connects Shopify; appointment times and titles where a Customer connects a calendar; and review text and approved replies where a Customer connects its Google Business Profile and the feature is enabled.

In Regulated Industries, Customer Personal Data may include sensitive information, such as patient records, children's details, legal matters and financial or insurance details. These are kept in private fields that the AI Receptionist never reads aloud, and call transcripts in those industries are never shown to staff.

### 3.3 Technical and security data

Network address and browser of people who sign in or visit our pages, security and audit logs of privileged actions, and rate limit records. Our logs do not hold full telephone numbers, email addresses, message contents or transcripts, and what must appear is masked.

## 5. Artificial intelligence

5.1 Zella Voice uses artificial intelligence to hold telephone conversations, to transcribe and summarize calls and, where enabled, to suggest knowledge answers and review replies and to classify calls by topic.

5.2 **Callers are always told.** At the start of every call, the AI Receptionist says it is an AI receptionist for the Customer. A Customer cannot remove this disclosure.

5.3 **Limits.** The AI Receptionist answers only from the Customer's approved knowledge and checked tools, takes a message when it does not know, gives no medical, legal, financial or safety advice, and tells callers in an emergency to call 911.

5.4 **Minimum data.** The AI model receives only the conversation and business context needed for the task, and never another Customer's data or any secrets.

5.5 **Human review.** Suggested answers and replies are drafts until a person at the Customer approves them.

5.6 **No significant automated decisions.** The AI Receptionist acts at the caller's request and within the Customer's rules. It does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about a person.

Schedule 2 (AI and Call Disclosure Policy) of the Zella Voice Terms of Service gives more detail.

## 6. Call recording and transcripts

6.1 **Recording is off by default.** Call recording becomes available only after recording consent rules and caller disclosures have been legally reviewed. When a Customer has recording on, callers hear "This call is recorded." at the start of the call. The Customer is responsible for any consent the law requires.

6.2 **Transcripts** are visible only to a Customer's owners, admins and managers, to staff only if the Owner allows it, and never to staff in Regulated Industries.

6.3 Recordings and transcripts are deleted 90 days after the call, or earlier when they are erased, at the end of the export window after cancellation, or on account deletion (sections 10 and 11). If a healthcare Customer has asked, under its business associate agreement, for a transcript or recording to be returned before it is deleted, its deletion is suspended until it is returned, for a limited period.

## 7. Messages, consent and opting out

7.1 Before every call or message, the Services check consent for that channel and purpose (service or marketing), opt outs and do not contact flags, contact hours in the recipient's time zone when known, otherwise the Customer's, frequency limits and duplicates (Zella Voice Messaging Terms, sections 4.1 and 4.2). Consent is recorded with when and how it was given.

7.2 Reply **STOP** to a text to stop texts from that Customer, use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email, or say "don't call me" to stop calls. Opt outs take effect immediately for that Customer. We keep the opt out record after other data is erased so that it keeps working. The Zella Voice Messaging Terms give more detail.

## 11. Export, erasure and account deletion

11.1 **Export.** For a request from one End User, the Customer's owner or admin produces a file of that person's records only, including call summaries and transcripts; as in every export, the transcripts of Customers in Regulated Industries are left out. Owners, admins and managers can export customers and call history as CSV files at any time; the call history file holds call details and outcomes but no transcripts, summaries or recordings. The Owner can also request a whole business export of all records and files, downloadable for 7 days; recording links, and transcripts of Customers in Regulated Industries, are left out of the whole business export. **[COUNSEL CHOICE: (A) EXCLUDE TRANSCRIPTS OF CUSTOMERS IN REGULATED INDUSTRIES FROM EVERY EXPORT, INCLUDING AFTER CANCELLATION (CURRENT TEXT); OR (B) LET THE OWNER EXPORT THEM WITH SAFEGUARDS, AND UPDATE SECTIONS 10 AND 11.1, DPA 9.1 AND 12.2 AND TERMS 12.6 TO MATCH]**

11.2 **Erasing an End User.** A Customer can erase an End User. Their details, messages and calls are removed, including transcripts, recordings and summaries, and for each call that is not under a legal hold or named in a pending return request a request to delete the call at Vapi is sent. Their consent and opt out history is kept, data under a legal hold (including the copy of a held call at Vapi) is kept, and not deleted at Vapi, until the hold is released, only a transcript or recording named in a pending return request under a healthcare Customer's business associate agreement (including the copy at Vapi) is kept until it is returned, and for no longer than the limited period in DPA section 12.1, and is then deleted, while the rest of the erasure goes ahead (for a call under both a legal hold and a return request, DPA Schedule 1, section S1.6.3(e) applies **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THE PRECEDENCE WHERE A CALL IS UNDER BOTH A LEGAL HOLD AND A RETURN REQUEST]**), and data the law requires us to keep is kept for as long as required. Erasure needs the approval of a second owner or admin when the Customer has two or more active owners or admins; a Customer with only one owner or admin has no second approver, and the erasure is still recorded in the audit log.

11.3 **Deleting an account.** The Owner requests deletion in Settings and Zella Voice confirms it. The account is frozen immediately (calls are no longer answered and automations stop) and is deleted 30 days later unless the Owner withdraws the request. Stored files are removed, and for data not under a legal hold or a pending return request a request to delete the copies held by Vapi is sent; Vapi deletes them where technically possible. If a healthcare Customer has asked, under its business associate agreement, for a transcript or recording to be returned and the request is still pending, that transcript or recording, including the copy held by Vapi, is kept until it is returned, and for no longer than the limited period in DPA sections 12.1 and 12.3, and is then deleted. For a call under both a legal hold and a return request, DPA Schedule 1, section S1.6.3(e) applies. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THE PRECEDENCE WHERE A CALL IS UNDER BOTH A LEGAL HOLD AND A RETURN REQUEST]** Audit history is kept without the Customer's details, consent and opt out history is kept so that opt outs continue to work, data under a legal hold (including any copy at Vapi) is kept, and not deleted at Vapi, until the hold is released, and data the law requires us to keep is kept for as long as required.

## 13. Security

13.1 Every Customer's data is kept separate by row level security on every table and server checks of each person's membership and role. Access follows the role. Every Zella Voice staff member uses two step sign in. Erasing a customer needs a second owner's or admin's approval where the Customer has two or more, and privileged actions are recorded in an append only audit log. Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Secrets are kept only in secure stores. Files are kept in private storage and checked after upload, and private links open one item only and expire. Backups allow point in time recovery. Annex 2 of the DPA lists the measures in full.

13.2 No system is completely secure. If a security incident affects Personal Data, we will notify affected Customers and, where the law requires, individuals and authorities.

## 14. Children

The Services are for businesses and are not directed to children. Authorized Users must be adults. A Customer such as a childcare provider may keep children's details that a parent or guardian provides; the Customer is responsible for that data, and the Services keep it in private fields. If you believe a child has given us Personal Data directly, contact us and we will delete it.

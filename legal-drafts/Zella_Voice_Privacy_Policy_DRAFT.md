## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Zella Voice is an AI phone receptionist and customer management system (CRM) for small businesses such as restaurants, salons, clinics and trade services. An AI receptionist answers the business's calls, answers questions from information the business has approved, books and changes appointments, takes messages and hands calls to a person when it should. Everything it does is recorded in the business's own CRM.

Zella Voice is provided by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company, of **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]** ("Zella Voice", "we", "us"). Zella Voice is currently offered to businesses in the United States.

This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, who helps us, how long we keep it and what choices people have. It covers:

- people who sign in to Zella Voice to run a business (business owners and their team members);
- people who call, text, email or book with a business that uses Zella Voice (callers, clients, patients, guests and leads);
- people who visit our public pages, such as /status, /privacy and /terms, or who open a private link we send, such as a reschedule or unsubscribe link.

## 2. Our two roles

**For a business's own customers, the business is in charge.** A business that uses Zella Voice (our customer) decides why and how information about its callers and clients is used. For that information the business is the controller (in some laws, the "business" or "data fiduciary") and Zella Voice processes it on the business's behalf, as its processor or service provider, under our agreement with the business and our **[DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM LOCATION]**.

If you called, texted or booked with a business that uses Zella Voice, please contact that business first about your information. The business's own privacy notice applies to you. We help the business answer your request (see section 12).

**For the people who run a business on Zella Voice, we are in charge.** For business account information, such as an owner's sign in details, identity checks, billing records and security logs, Zella Voice is the controller.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Business account information

- Name, email address, phone number and role in the business (owner, admin, manager, staff or viewer).
- Sign in records: password (stored only in a protected form by our sign in provider), two step sign in (authenticator app) records, sign in times, and the devices that are signed in.
- Invitations to join a business, and acceptance of these documents (which version, when and by whom).

### 3.2 Business information and settings

- Business name, address, industry, opening hours, holidays, services, prices the business chooses to enter, staff, locations, phone numbers and transfer numbers, and a fallback number for when the AI receptionist cannot answer.
- The receptionist's greeting, voice, languages, call handling rules and approved knowledge (questions and answers the business has approved, and pages from the business's own website when the business asks us to read them).
- Automations, message templates, integrations the business connects, and its plan and usage.

### 3.3 Information about a business's customers

A business keeps records about the people it serves. Depending on the business and its industry these can include:

- names, phone numbers, email addresses, preferred language and time zone;
- appointments, reservations, jobs, waitlist entries, check ins and attendance;
- requests, messages, notes, tasks and follow ups;
- records the business's industry needs (for example pets, vehicles, jobs or matters), which may include files the business uploads;
- relationships the business records (for example a parent or guardian) and what each person may do;
- consent and contact preferences, opt outs and do not contact flags;
- money records the business keeps, such as charges, quotes and payments recorded. No card numbers are stored in Zella Voice.

Some businesses work in industries where information can be sensitive, such as healthcare, dental, childcare, legal, financial services and insurance. We keep sensitive fields (for example access codes, member numbers and children's details) apart from other fields, the receptionist never reads them aloud, and staff in those industries never see call transcripts. Regulated industries launch only after legal review.

### 3.4 Calls

For each call the AI receptionist handles, we keep:

- the calling and called numbers, the time and length of the call and how it ended;
- what the receptionist did (for example the appointment it booked or the message it took), the outcome, and a written summary;
- a transcript of the conversation;
- a recording, **only** when the business has turned call recording on. Call recording is off by default and stays off until recording consent and caller disclosures have been legally reviewed (see section 6).

We identify a caller by the number the phone network gives us, never by what is said on the call. Before the receptionist shares or changes a booking it may ask for the name on the booking, the date, or the booking's confirmation code.

### 3.5 Messages

Texts and emails sent or received for the business (for example appointment confirmations, reminders and follow ups), when they were sent, whether they were delivered, and replies. Text and email sending each has its own switch and is off until it is turned on for live use.

### 3.6 Leads and connected services

When a business connects them, we receive lead details from its website forms and chat widget, Google Ads lead forms and, where enabled, Facebook and Instagram lead ads. When a business connects a calendar (Google or Microsoft), we read busy times and write the business's bookings. When an online store connects Shopify, the receptionist can look up an order's status. When a business connects its Google Business Profile, where enabled, we read reviews and post only replies the owner has approved.

### 3.7 Owner identity checks

Before a business goes live, the owner verifies their identity by uploading a photo ID and the business's registration. These documents are kept in private storage and checked by the Zella Voice team. Where enabled, an identity verification provider may carry out this check instead (see section 8).

### 3.8 Billing information

The business's plan, price, allowances, usage, invoices, refunds and payment status. Card details are entered on our payment provider's own pages and **never reach Zella Voice**.

### 3.9 Technical and security information

The network address and browser of people who sign in, security and audit logs of privileged actions (who did what, when, in which business), and limits on repeated attempts. Our logs are not a data store: they do not hold full phone numbers, emails, message contents or transcripts, and we mask what must appear.

## 5. How AI is used

Zella Voice uses AI to hold phone conversations, to transcribe and summarize calls and, where enabled, to suggest answers for the business's knowledge base, to suggest replies to reviews and to sort calls by topic.

- **Callers are always told.** At the start of every call the receptionist says it is an AI receptionist for the business. A business can change its greeting but cannot remove this disclosure.
- **The receptionist works only from the business's approved information and server checked tools.** If it does not know an answer, it says so and takes a message. It never gives medical, legal, financial or safety advice, and it tells callers in an emergency to call 911.
- **Only what the task needs.** The AI model receives the conversation and the business context needed for the call or task. It never receives another business's data, secrets or other customers' details.
- **People stay in charge.** Suggested knowledge answers and review replies are drafts: nothing is published until the owner, or someone the owner allows, approves it. The receptionist's actions are logged so the business can check them.
- **No automated decisions with legal effects.** The receptionist books, moves and cancels appointments at the caller's request and within the business's rules. It does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about a person.
- **No training.** Our AI model provider receives information only to perform the task and does not use it to train its models.

## 6. Call recording, transcripts and disclosures

- **Recording is off by default.** Call recording is turned on only per business, only after recording consent rules and caller disclosures have been legally reviewed and approved by Zella Voice. When a business has recording on, callers hear "This call is recorded." at the start of the call.
- **Transcripts.** The conversation is transcribed so the business can see what was said and so a summary can be written. Transcripts can be seen only by owners, admins and managers, by staff only if the owner allows it, and never by staff in sensitive industries.
- **Retention.** Recordings and transcripts are kept for 90 days after the call and then deleted automatically (section 10).
- The business is responsible for telling its callers about its use of Zella Voice, and for any consent the law requires where it and its callers are.

## 7. Messages, consent and opting out

Every message passes the same checks before it is sent: the person's consent for that channel and purpose (service or marketing), opt outs, contact hours in the recipient's time zone, frequency limits and duplicate protection. Consent is recorded with when and how it was given. Opt outs are honored at once and apply to the business the person opted out of.

- Reply **STOP** to a text to stop texts from that business. Reply **HELP** for help.
- Use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email to stop marketing emails from that business.
- Tell the receptionist or the business "don't call me" to stop its calls.

We keep the record of an opt out even after other information is erased, so that the opt out keeps working.

## 10. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes in section 4, then delete or anonymize it. Our standard periods are below. Anything under an active legal hold (for example because of a legal claim or an investigation) is kept until the hold is released, and then deleted on schedule.

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Call recordings (only when recording is on) | 90 days after the call, then deleted automatically |
| Call transcripts | 90 days after the call, then removed automatically |
| Copies of calls held by Vapi | Deleted at Vapi once the recording and transcript have been removed from Zella Voice, or when a customer or account is erased |
| Text and email contents | 2 years, then the words are removed and only the record that a message was sent stays |
| Audit and security logs | 7 years, unless the law requires otherwise. They hold no message contents, transcripts or recordings |
| Owner identity documents | 90 days after the check; the result of the check is kept. A document replaced by a new copy is removed after 30 days |
| A business's other CRM data, call summaries and settings | While the account is active |
| After a business cancels | Available for export for 30 days after cancellation, then deleted. Backups are cleared within 90 days |
| Phone numbers of a cancelled business | Released 30 days after cancellation, unless ported out |
| Opt out and consent records | Kept after erasure so that opt outs keep working |

Some industries may need longer periods for certain records, and a business may choose shorter periods within what the platform allows.

## 11. Export, erasure and account deletion

- **Exporting.** Owners can export their customers and call history as CSV at any time. An owner can also ask Zella Voice for a whole business export (all of the business's records and files); it is downloadable for 7 days through private links. Recording links, and the transcripts of businesses in sensitive industries, are left out of that export.
- **Erasing one person.** A business can erase one of its customers. Their details, messages and calls are removed (including transcripts, recordings and summaries), and a request to delete the call at Vapi is sent. Their opt out and consent history is kept so that opt outs keep working. Erasure needs a second person's approval in the business.
- **Deleting a business account.** The owner asks in Settings, and a Zella Voice admin confirms the request. The account is frozen at once (calls are no longer answered and automations stop) and everything is deleted 30 days later, unless the owner cancels the request in that time. Stored files are removed and copies at Vapi are deleted. Audit history is kept without the business's details.
- **Cancelling.** When a business cancels, its data stays available for export for 30 days and is then deleted, after a final offer to export.

## 13. Security

- **Every business's data is kept apart.** Row security is on for every table, every business function checks the person's membership and role, and we test that one business can never reach another's data.
- **Access is limited.** People see only what their role allows. Every Zella Voice staff member uses two step sign in, and access by our staff is logged and can be seen by the business.
- **Risky actions need a second confirmation**, such as exports, erasure and role changes, and every privileged action is written to an append only audit log.
- **Encryption.** Information travels over encrypted connections. **[CONFIRM ENCRYPTION AT REST WITH THE HOSTING PROVIDER]**
- **Secrets** such as provider keys are kept only in secure stores, never in code or logs.
- **Private files and links.** Files are kept in private storage, checked after upload, and private links for customers open one item only and expire.
- **Backups** allow point in time recovery.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects personal information, we will tell the affected businesses and, where the law requires, the people and authorities concerned.

## 14. Children

Zella Voice is a service for businesses and is not directed to children. Business accounts are for adults only. Some businesses, such as childcare providers, may keep information about children that a parent or guardian gives them; that business is responsible for it, and Zella Voice keeps children's details in private fields that the receptionist never reads aloud. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

## 15. Cookies and browser storage

The Zella Voice web app does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's storage for things the app needs to work:

- keeping you signed in (stored by our sign in provider);
- remembering the business you last opened, which menu groups you folded, your chosen look and background, your notification pop up, sound and desktop alert choices, and which usage notices you have dismissed;
- completing a security step, such as recording a newly added authenticator.

These items stay on your device. You can clear them in your browser at any time; you will then be signed out and your display choices will return to their defaults. The web app loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your network address and browser details when a page loads. **[CONFIRM WHETHER A COOKIE BANNER OR COOKIE POLICY IS REQUIRED]**

## Part A. Mutual Non Disclosure Agreement

### A1. Parties and purpose

A1.1 This Mutual Non Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is between **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company of **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]** ("Zella Voice"), and **[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL NAME]** of **[COUNTERPARTY ADDRESS]** ("Counterparty"), each a "party".

A1.2 The parties wish to share information to evaluate or pursue **[PURPOSE OF DISCLOSURE]** (the "Purpose").

A1.3 This NDA takes effect on **[NDA EFFECTIVE DATE, OR IF LEFT BLANK, THE DATE OF THE LAST SIGNATURE BELOW]** (the "Effective Date").

### A2. Confidential Information

A2.1 "Confidential Information" means nonpublic information that a party ("Discloser") discloses to the other ("Recipient"), in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including product plans, source code, designs, prompts, pricing, customer and business information and Personal Data.

A2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of Recipient; (b) Recipient knew without restriction before receiving it; (c) Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction; or (d) Recipient develops independently without using Discloser's Confidential Information.

### A3. Obligations

A3.1 Recipient must use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as this NDA. Recipient is responsible for their compliance.

A3.2 Recipient may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, after giving prompt notice where legally permitted and reasonable help, at Discloser's cost, to seek protection.

A3.3 Recipient must not reverse engineer, decompile or analyze any software, model or prototype Discloser provides, and must not use Confidential Information to train artificial intelligence models.

A3.4 **Personal Data.** Neither party will disclose Personal Data under this NDA unless necessary for the Purpose and agreed in writing in advance. Any Personal Data received must be protected, used only for the Purpose and deleted when no longer needed.

### A4. Term, return and remedies

A4.1 This NDA lasts **[NDA TERM]** from the Effective Date in section A1.3. Obligations for each disclosure last **[CONFIDENTIALITY PERIOD]** after the disclosure, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

A4.2 On request, Recipient must return or destroy Confidential Information, except copies kept under a routine backup or required by law, which remain confidential.

A4.3 Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm, and Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

A4.4 **Whistleblower notice.** Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b), an individual is not liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

### A5. General

A5.1 No license or other right is granted except as stated. Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither party must enter into any further agreement.

A5.2 This NDA is governed by **[GOVERNING LAW AND COURTS]**. It is the entire agreement about its subject matter and may be signed electronically and in counterparts.

## Part B. Contractor Agreement

### B1. Parties and services

B1.1 This Contractor Agreement is between **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Zella Voice"), and **[CONTRACTOR LEGAL NAME]** of **[CONTRACTOR ADDRESS]** ("Contractor").

B1.2 Contractor will provide the services described in each statement of work signed by both parties (the "Services"), the first of which is **[FIRST STATEMENT OF WORK REFERENCE]**.

B1.3 Contractor is an independent contractor, controls the manner of performing the Services, provides its own equipment unless agreed otherwise, and is responsible for its own taxes, insurance and benefits. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM CLASSIFICATION FOR THE CONTRACTOR'S LOCATION]**

### B2. Fees

Zella Voice will pay the fees in each statement of work within **[PAYMENT TERMS]** after receiving a correct invoice. Expenses are reimbursed only if approved in writing in advance.

### B3. Confidentiality and data

B3.1 **Confidentiality.** "Confidential Information" means all nonpublic information of Zella Voice and its customers that Contractor receives or creates in performing the Services, including product plans, source code, designs, prompts, security information, customer data and business information, but not information that is or becomes public through no fault of Contractor, that Contractor knew without restriction before receiving it, or that Contractor lawfully receives from a third party without restriction. Contractor must use Confidential Information only to perform the Services (the "Purpose"), protect it with at least reasonable care, disclose it to no one without Zella Voice's written consent unless the law requires it (after giving prompt notice where permitted), and return or destroy it when the engagement ends. These obligations last during the engagement and for **[CONTRACTOR CONFIDENTIALITY PERIOD]** afterward, and for trade secrets and customer data for as long as they remain trade secrets or are held. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b), an individual is not liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

B3.2 Contractor must follow Zella Voice's security rules, including: never copying secrets, credentials or customer data into code, chat, tickets, documents or personal devices; using two step sign in; never making real calls, texts or emails, turning on a live switch, changing a live voice assistant or telephone number, or changing production systems, without the explicit written approval of the Zella Voice owner for that specific action (an approval from anyone else, or a general approval, is not enough); and reporting any suspected security incident immediately.

B3.3 Where Contractor processes Personal Data for Zella Voice, it does so only on Zella Voice's written instructions and under the data protection terms Zella Voice requires. **[ATTACH DATA PROCESSING TERMS IF CONTRACTOR WILL ACCESS PERSONAL DATA]**

### B4. Intellectual property

B4.1 **Assignment.** Contractor hereby assigns to Zella Voice all right, title and interest in all work product, software, documentation, designs, prompts, data, inventions and other materials that Contractor creates for Zella Voice under this agreement, and all intellectual property rights in them (the "Work Product"), with effect from creation. To the extent any Work Product qualifies as a "work made for hire" under the US Copyright Act, it is a work made for hire owned by Zella Voice.

B4.2 **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Contractor waives, and agrees not to assert, any moral rights or similar rights in the Work Product.

B4.3 **Further assurance.** Contractor will sign documents and take actions reasonably requested to perfect Zella Voice's rights, at Zella Voice's expense, and appoints Zella Voice as its attorney in fact for that limited purpose if Contractor does not do so.

B4.4 **Prior inventions.** Contractor's pre existing materials listed in Schedule B1 ("Prior Inventions") remain Contractor's. If Contractor includes a Prior Invention in the Work Product, Contractor grants Zella Voice a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty free, transferable license to use, modify and sublicense it as part of the Work Product.

B4.5 **Open source and third party materials.** Contractor must not include any open source or third party material in the Work Product without Zella Voice's prior written approval of the specific material and its license. Contractor must never include material under a license that would require Zella Voice to disclose or license its own source code, or that would restrict its commercial use, unless Zella Voice approves it in writing.

B4.6 **No AI training.** Contractor must not input Zella Voice's confidential information or customer data into any external artificial intelligence service that may use it for training.

### B5. Warranties and term

B5.1 Contractor warrants that the Work Product is original or properly licensed, does not infringe third party rights to Contractor's knowledge, and that Contractor is free to enter into this agreement.

B5.2 Either party may end this agreement on **[TERMINATION NOTICE PERIOD]** written notice. On termination Contractor must deliver all Work Product and return or destroy Zella Voice's information. Sections B3, B4 and B5.1 survive.

B5.3 This agreement is governed by **[GOVERNING LAW AND COURTS]** and may be signed electronically.

## Part C. Employee Confidentiality and Invention Assignment Agreement

**[COUNSEL NOTE: several states limit invention assignment for employees, for example California Labor Code section 2870, and similar statutes in other states such as Delaware, Illinois, Minnesota, North Carolina and Washington. Some states also limit non competition and non solicitation covenants. This template contains no non compete. Counsel must adapt it to the employee's state before use.]**

### C1. Parties

This agreement is between **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Zella Voice"), and **[EMPLOYEE NAME]** ("Employee"), in consideration of Employee's employment and the compensation paid to Employee.

### C2. Confidentiality

C2.1 During and after employment, Employee must keep confidential and use only for Zella Voice's business all nonpublic information of Zella Voice and its customers, including product plans, source code, designs, prompts, security information, customer data and business information.

C2.2 Employee must follow Zella Voice's security rules, including: never copying secrets, credentials or customer data into code, chat, tickets, documents or personal devices; using two step sign in; never making real calls, texts or emails, turning on a live switch, changing a live voice assistant or telephone number, or changing production systems, without the explicit written approval of the Zella Voice owner for that specific action; and reporting any suspected security incident immediately. On leaving, Employee must return all Zella Voice property and information and delete copies from personal devices.

C2.3 **Whistleblower notice.** Nothing in this agreement prevents Employee from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency, from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws, or from discussing wages or working conditions as the law protects. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b), an individual is not liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

### C3. Inventions

C3.1 **Assignment.** Employee hereby assigns to Zella Voice all right, title and interest in all inventions, works of authorship, software, designs, prompts and other developments that Employee creates, alone or with others, during employment and that relate to Zella Voice's business or actual or anticipated research, or result from work performed for Zella Voice, or use Zella Voice's equipment, supplies, facilities or confidential information (the "Company Inventions"), and all intellectual property rights in them. Works of authorship created within the scope of employment are works made for hire.

C3.2 **Statutory exclusion.** This assignment does not apply to an invention that qualifies for exclusion under California Labor Code section 2870 or a similar law that applies to Employee, which is set out in Exhibit C2. **[COUNSEL TO ATTACH THE APPLICABLE STATUTORY TEXT AS EXHIBIT C2]**

C3.3 **Disclosure, moral rights and further assurance.** Employee will promptly disclose Company Inventions to Zella Voice, waives moral rights in them to the extent permitted by law, and will sign documents reasonably requested to perfect Zella Voice's rights.

C3.4 **Prior inventions.** "Employee Prior Inventions" means the inventions, works of authorship, software and other developments that Employee owns, alone or jointly, that were made before Employee's employment with Zella Voice began, and that are listed in Exhibit C1. Employee Prior Inventions are not Company Inventions and remain Employee's. If none are listed in Exhibit C1, Employee represents that there are none. Employee must not incorporate an Employee Prior Invention into any Company Invention, product or service of Zella Voice without Zella Voice's prior written consent. If Employee does incorporate one, with or without consent, Employee grants Zella Voice a non exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, fully paid, royalty free, transferable license, with the right to sublicense, to make, use, sell, copy, modify, distribute and otherwise exploit that Employee Prior Invention as part of, or in connection with, that Company Invention, product or service.

C3.5 **Open source and third party materials.** Employee must not include any open source or third party material in a Company Invention, product or service of Zella Voice without Zella Voice's prior written approval of the specific material and its license, and must never include material under a license that would require Zella Voice to disclose or license its own source code, or that would restrict its commercial use, unless Zella Voice approves it in writing.

### C4. General

C4.1 This agreement does not change the at will nature of employment, where applicable. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM FOR THE EMPLOYEE'S STATE]**

C4.2 This agreement is governed by **[EMPLOYEE GOVERNING LAW]** and may be signed electronically.

## Part E. Reseller and Referral Partner Agreement

### E1. Parties and model

E1.1 This agreement is between **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Zella Voice"), and **[PARTNER LEGAL NAME]** of **[PARTNER ADDRESS]** ("Partner").

E1.2 **Model.** The parties select one model: **[REFERRAL OR RESALE]**.

- **Referral:** Partner introduces prospective customers to Zella Voice. Zella Voice contracts with, onboards and bills each customer directly under the Zella Voice Terms of Service. Partner earns a referral commission.
- **Resale:** Partner buys subscriptions from Zella Voice and resells them to its own customers at prices Partner sets. Each end customer must still accept the Zella Voice Terms of Service, including the Data Processing Addendum, before using the Services, and Zella Voice may refuse any end customer. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THE RESALE STRUCTURE, INCLUDING WHO BILLS AND WHO SUPPORTS THE END CUSTOMER]**

E1.3 Partner is not exclusive, and Zella Voice may appoint other partners and sell directly in any territory.

### E2. Referrals and commissions

E2.1 A referral is "qualified" when Partner registers it in writing before Zella Voice has had contact with the prospect, and the prospect signs an Order Form within **[REFERRAL WINDOW]** after registration.

E2.2 For each qualified referral, Zella Voice pays Partner **[COMMISSION RATE OR AMOUNT]** of the Plan Fees actually received from that customer for **[COMMISSION PERIOD]**, excluding taxes, setup fees, packs, refunds, credits and chargebacks. Commissions are paid **[COMMISSION PAYMENT TIMING]** and are reversed for any amount later refunded.

E2.3 For resale, Partner pays Zella Voice **[RESALE PRICE OR DISCOUNT]** within **[PAYMENT TERMS]**, whether or not Partner is paid by its customer.

### E3. Partner obligations

E3.1 Partner must describe the Services accurately, using only Zella Voice's current materials, and must not make any statement about features, compliance, availability or results that Zella Voice has not approved in writing. In particular, Partner must not say that the Services are HIPAA compliant, an emergency service, or able to give professional advice.

E3.2 **No authority to bind.** Partner is an independent contractor and has no authority to make commitments, accept orders or grant terms on behalf of Zella Voice.

E3.3 **Compliance.** Partner must comply with all applicable laws in its marketing and sales, including the TCPA, the CAN SPAM Act, state telemarketing laws and do not call rules, and anti bribery laws including the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act. Partner must not offer or give anything of value to improperly influence any person.

E3.4 **Customer data.** Customer Data belongs to each customer and is governed by the customer's agreement with Zella Voice, including the Data Processing Addendum. Partner receives no access to Customer Data unless the customer authorizes it in the Services, and then only for that customer's purposes.

### E4. Brand and trademarks

E4.1 Zella Voice grants Partner a non exclusive, non transferable, revocable license during the term to use the Zella Voice name and logo only to promote the Services, following Zella Voice's brand guidelines, including writing the brand as "Zella Voice" in full. Partner must not register any name, domain or social media handle containing "Zella Voice" or anything confusingly similar.

E4.2 Each party may use the other's name in a customer or partner list only with prior written consent.

### E5. Term and termination

E5.1 This agreement lasts **[PARTNER AGREEMENT TERM]** and renews for successive periods of the same length unless either party gives **[NON RENEWAL NOTICE PERIOD]** notice.

E5.2 Either party may terminate on **[TERMINATION NOTICE PERIOD]** written notice, or immediately for material breach that is not cured within 30 days, or for a breach of section E3.3. Commissions earned before termination remain payable; no commissions accrue after termination **[UNLESS COUNSEL AGREES A TAIL PERIOD]**.

E5.3 On termination Partner must stop using Zella Voice's marks and return or destroy Zella Voice's confidential information. Resale customers continue under their own agreements with Zella Voice. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM TRANSITION OF RESALE CUSTOMERS]**

### E6. Confidentiality, liability and general

E6.1 **Confidentiality.** Each party must keep confidential the nonpublic information the other discloses under this agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, except information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, that the recipient already knew without restriction, that the recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction, or that the recipient develops independently. Each party must use the other's confidential information only to perform this agreement (the "Purpose"), protect it with at least reasonable care, disclose it only to its personnel and advisers who need it and are bound by similar obligations, and return or destroy it when this agreement ends. These obligations last during the term and for **[PARTNER CONFIDENTIALITY PERIOD]** afterward, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets. Partner receives no customer data except under section E3.4.

E6.2 Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the commissions or fees paid or payable under it in the 12 months before the claim, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, except for breach of sections E3.3, E3.4 or E4, confidentiality, or indemnity obligations. **[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM LIABILITY TERMS]**

E6.3 Partner will indemnify Zella Voice against claims arising from Partner's statements about the Services or Partner's breach of section E3.

E6.4 **Non solicitation (optional).** **[OPTIONAL CLAUSE: USE ONLY IF COUNSEL APPROVES]** During the term and for **[NON SOLICITATION PERIOD]** afterward, neither party will solicit for employment the other's employees with whom it worked under this agreement, except through general advertising. **[END OF OPTIONAL CLAUSE]**

E6.5 This agreement is governed by **[GOVERNING LAW AND COURTS]** and may be signed electronically.
